# BinkSuite Etsy shop makeover pack

Theme: cosy, warm and playful. Name used in public: **Bink** (no personal details).
Everything you paste is below. Pictures are in this folder.

Where things live on Etsy: **Shop Manager** (the little shop icon on etsy.com when you're signed in) → **Settings** → **Info & appearance**.
Etsy moves its menus around sometimes, so if something isn't where these steps say, send Claude a screenshot.

## Step 5: Shop policies

Shop Manager → Settings → **Policies** (sometimes called "Shop policies").

**Returns and exchanges:** choose **"I don't accept returns, exchanges or cancellations"** for digital items, and paste this in the extra info box:

```
Because these are digital downloads, I can't accept returns or exchanges once a file has been downloaded.
If a file is faulty, won't open, or isn't what the listing described, message me within 30 days and I'll fix it, send a new file, or refund you.
UK & EU buyers: by buying a digital download you agree to it being delivered straight away, which means the 14-day right to cancel ends once the download starts. This doesn't affect your rights if a file is faulty.
```

**Digital downloads / FAQ:** add these as FAQs:

```
Q: Will I get a physical item?
A: No. This is a digital download. Nothing is posted to you. You'll get your files straight away after payment, from your Etsy Purchases page and in your confirmation email.

Q: How do I print my files?
A: Print at home on good paper or card, or upload the file to a local print shop or an online printer. For best results choose "actual size" or "100%" in your print settings.

Q: Can I sell or share the files?
A: The files are for your own personal use. Please don't resell, share or give them away, digitally or printed.

Q: Colours look different when printed?
A: Screens and printers show colour slightly differently, so small changes are normal.
```

**Privacy policy:** Etsy offers a ready-made privacy policy. Turn it on, then add this to it:

```
I only use your name, email and order details to deliver your order and reply to your messages. I never add you to a mailing list or use your details for marketing, and I never see or store your payment details. You can ask me to see, correct or delete the information I hold about you at any time by messaging me on Etsy.
```
